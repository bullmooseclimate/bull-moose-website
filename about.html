Let's Talk

Comfort starts at home

Your home should be a place where you can relax and feel comfortable in every season. We believe choosing a heating and cooling system should be straightforward, informed, and centered on what actually works for your home.

Bull Moose Climate Systems LLC focuses on heat pump solutions for homeowners looking to improve comfort and make thoughtful investments in their homes.

Our approach

Listen First

Every home and homeowner is different. We start by understanding what you want to improve, how you use your space, and what matters most to you.

Practical Recommendations

We focus on solutions that make sense for the home's layout, heating needs, and budget rather than assuming one system works for everyone.

Careful Work

Details matter. Equipment placement, installation quality, and proper system operation all contribute to long-term comfort.

Clear Communication

You deserve to understand your options, what the work involves, and how to operate and care for your equipment.

Focused on your home

Whether you're considering a single room, several living spaces, or a larger heating and cooling upgrade, our goal is to help you make a confident decision.

We work with heat pump solutions suited to Maine homes and the demands of our changing seasons.
