One system. Multiple comfort zones.

A multi-zone heat pump connects multiple indoor units to a compatible outdoor unit. Each indoor unit serves a particular room or area, allowing occupants to adjust temperatures independently.

Multi-zone ductless mini-split systems can be useful for Maine homes with several living areas, different comfort preferences, or rooms that are difficult to heat and cool evenly.

Comfort for Maine's changing seasons

Cold-climate heat pumps are designed to provide heating even when outdoor temperatures drop. Selecting equipment suited to Maine's winter conditions is important when planning a multi-zone system.

System capacity and heating performance can vary with outdoor temperature and the combination of indoor units. Proper equipment selection helps ensure the system is appropriate for the home's heating needs.

Planning the right multi-zone system

A successful installation starts with understanding the home. Room sizes, insulation, windows, layout, and heating requirements all affect equipment selection.

The outdoor unit must be properly matched to the indoor units. Equipment capacity, refrigerant piping limits, and manufacturer-approved combinations must be considered before installation.
