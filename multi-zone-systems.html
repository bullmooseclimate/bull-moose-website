One system. Multiple comfort zones.

A multi-zone heat pump connects multiple indoor units to a compatible outdoor unit. Each indoor unit serves a particular room or area, allowing occupants to adjust temperatures independently.

This can be useful for homes with several living areas, different comfort preferences, or rooms that are difficult to heat and cool evenly.

Planning a multi-zone system

A successful installation starts with understanding the home. Room sizes, insulation, windows, layout, and heating requirements all affect the design.

The outdoor unit must be properly matched to the indoor units. Equipment capacity, piping limits, and manufacturer-approved combinations must be considered before installation.
