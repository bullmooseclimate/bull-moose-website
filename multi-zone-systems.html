One system. Multiple comfort zones.

A multi-zone heat pump connects multiple indoor units to a compatible outdoor unit. Each indoor unit serves a particular room or area, allowing occupants to adjust temperatures independently.

This can be a useful solution for homes with several living areas, different comfort preferences, or rooms that are difficult to heat and cool evenly.

Individual Room Controls

Each indoor unit has its own temperature setting, helping different areas maintain the comfort level their occupants prefer.

Flexible Indoor Units

Depending on the equipment, indoor units may be available in different styles and capacities to suit the rooms being served.

Fewer Outdoor Units

Multiple compatible indoor units can connect to one outdoor unit, rather than requiring a separate outdoor unit for every room.

Customized Comfort

Rooms that receive different amounts of sunlight, have different uses, or are occupied at different times can have their own settings.

Planning a multi-zone system

A successful multi-zone installation starts with understanding the home. Room sizes, insulation, windows, layout, and heating requirements all affect the design.

The outdoor unit must be properly matched to the indoor units. Equipment capacity, piping limits, and manufacturer-approved combinations must be considered before installation.
