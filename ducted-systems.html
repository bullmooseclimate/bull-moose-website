Comfort throughout your home

Ducted heat pumps use an indoor air handler connected to an outdoor heat pump. The air handler moves conditioned air through ductwork to deliver heating and cooling to multiple rooms.

For homes with suitable existing ductwork, a ducted heat pump can be an excellent way to improve comfort while making use of the home's existing air distribution system.

Centralized Comfort

One central system distributes heated or cooled air through the home's ductwork.

Use Existing Ductwork

Existing ducts may be suitable, depending on their condition, size, layout, and airflow.

Heating and Air Conditioning

One system provides heating during colder months and cooling when temperatures rise.

Out-of-Sight Equipment

The indoor air handler is typically installed in a utility area, basement, attic, or other suitable location.

How the system works

Central heating and cooling

A ducted heat pump uses an outdoor unit and an indoor air handler to distribute conditioned air through the home.

Indoor Air Handler

The air handler moves heated or cooled air through the home's ductwork.

Outdoor Equipment

The outdoor unit works with the indoor air handler to provide heating and cooling.

Is a ducted heat pump right for your home?

Insulation, air leakage, window area, ceiling height, duct condition, and the home's heating needs all affect equipment selection.

Proper sizing and airflow are important. An evaluation helps determine whether existing ductwork can support the system.
