Whole-home heating and cooling

Ducted heat pumps use an indoor air handler connected to an outdoor heat pump. The air handler moves conditioned air through ductwork to deliver heating and cooling to multiple rooms.

For Maine homes with suitable existing ductwork, a ducted heat pump can provide an effective way to improve year-round comfort while making use of the home's existing air distribution system.

Centralized Comfort

One central system distributes heated or cooled air through the home's ductwork.

Existing Ductwork

Existing ducts may be suitable depending on their condition, size, layout, and airflow.

Heating and Air Conditioning

One system provides heating during colder months and cooling when temperatures rise.

Out-of-Sight Equipment

The indoor air handler is typically installed in a basement, utility area, attic, or other suitable location.

How the system works

Central heating and cooling

A ducted heat pump uses an outdoor unit and an indoor air handler to distribute conditioned air through the home.

Indoor Air Handler

The air handler moves heated or cooled air through the home's existing ductwork.

Outdoor Equipment

The outdoor unit works with the indoor air handler to provide efficient heating and cooling.

Designed for Maine's changing seasons

Modern cold-climate heat pumps are designed to provide heating in low outdoor temperatures. Choosing equipment appropriate for Maine's winter conditions is an important part of planning a system.

Proper equipment sizing and airflow are essential. Insulation, air leakage, window area, ceiling height, existing duct condition, and the home's heating needs all affect system selection.

Is a ducted heat pump right for your home?

A ducted system may be a good option if your home already has suitable ductwork and you want centralized heating and cooling throughout multiple rooms.

We evaluate the home's needs and existing setup to help determine whether a ducted heat pump is an appropriate fit. Our installation service does not include custom ductwork or duct modifications.
