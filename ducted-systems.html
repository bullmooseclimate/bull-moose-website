Comfort throughout your home

Ducted heat pumps use an indoor air handler connected to an outdoor heat pump. The air handler moves conditioned air through ductwork to deliver heating and cooling to multiple rooms.

For homes with suitable existing ductwork, a ducted heat pump can be an excellent way to improve comfort while making use of the home's existing air distribution system.

Centralized Comfort

One central system distributes heated or cooled air through the home's ductwork.

Use Existing Ductwork

Existing ducts may be suitable for a heat pump installation. Their condition, size, layout, and airflow should be evaluated before selecting equipment.

Heating and Air Conditioning

Enjoy heating during colder months and air conditioning when temperatures rise, all from one system.

Out-of-Sight Equipment

The indoor air handler is typically installed in a utility area, basement, attic, or other suitable location, with air delivered through registers.

Is a ducted heat pump right for your home?

The right system depends on more than square footage. Insulation, air leakage, window area, ceiling height, duct condition, and the home's heating needs all affect equipment selection.

Proper sizing and airflow are important. An evaluation helps determine whether existing ductwork can support the system or whether changes are needed.
