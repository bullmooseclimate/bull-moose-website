A simple way to improve home comfort

Ductless heat pumps provide heating and air conditioning through an indoor unit connected to an outdoor unit. They are a practical option for homes without existing ductwork, additions, finished rooms, and spaces that need better temperature control.

Heating and Cooling

One system provides both heating and air conditioning throughout the year.

No Ductwork Required

Indoor units connect to an outdoor heat pump through a small opening in the wall.

Room-by-Room Comfort

Each indoor unit can control the temperature in its own area.

Efficient Operation

Heat pumps move heat rather than creating it through conventional electric resistance heating.

Ductless options

One room or several?

Ductless systems can be configured for one main living area or multiple rooms throughout the home.

Single-Zone Systems

One indoor unit paired with one outdoor unit. Often a good fit for a main living area, addition, or individual room.

Multi-Zone Systems

Multiple indoor units connected to one compatible outdoor unit, providing separate comfort settings in several areas.
