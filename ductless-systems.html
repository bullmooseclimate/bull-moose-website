A simple way to improve home comfort

Ductless heat pumps provide heating and air conditioning through an indoor unit connected to an outdoor unit. They are a practical option for homes without existing ductwork, additions, finished rooms, and spaces that need better temperature control.

Heating and Cooling

One system provides both heating and air conditioning, helping keep your home comfortable throughout Maine's changing seasons.

No Ductwork Required

Indoor units connect to an outdoor heat pump through a small opening in the wall. This makes ductless systems useful for many existing homes.

Room-by-Room Comfort

Each indoor unit can control the temperature in its own area, allowing different rooms to have different comfort settings.

Efficient Operation

Heat pumps move heat rather than creating it through conventional electric resistance heating. The right equipment and proper installation make a difference.

Choosing the right ductless system

Every home is different. Room size, insulation, windows, layout, ceiling height, and existing heating equipment all matter when selecting a system.

Single-Zone Systems

A single indoor unit paired with one outdoor unit. Often a good fit for one main living area, an addition, or a room that needs dedicated heating and cooling.

Multi-Zone Systems

Multiple indoor units connected to one outdoor unit. This can provide separate temperature control in several rooms or areas.
