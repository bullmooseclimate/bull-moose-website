Explore available heat pump incentives

Efficiency Maine offers programs that may help eligible homeowners reduce the cost of qualifying heat pump installations. Incentive amounts, equipment requirements, eligibility, and program rules can change.

Before purchasing equipment or beginning an installation, review the current program requirements and confirm that your project qualifies.

What to consider before installing

Eligible Equipment

Not every heat pump automatically qualifies for an incentive. Equipment must meet the program's current requirements.

Installation Requirements

The program may require installation by an appropriately registered vendor and compliance with specific installation and documentation standards.

Home Heating Needs

System sizing, placement, existing heating equipment, and your home's insulation all matter when planning a successful heat pump installation.

Program Documentation

Keep required invoices, equipment information, installation records, and other documentation. Follow the current instructions for submitting an incentive claim.

Questions about your project?

We can discuss your home's heating and cooling needs and help you understand the installation options. Incentive eligibility and final approval are determined by Efficiency Maine under its current program rules.
