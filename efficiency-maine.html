What to Consider Before Installing a Heat Pump

Understanding the equipment and program requirements before starting your project can help avoid surprises.

Eligible Equipment

Not every heat pump automatically qualifies for an Efficiency Maine incentive. Equipment must meet the program's current requirements.

Installation Requirements

A program may require installation by an appropriately registered vendor and compliance with specific installation and documentation standards.

Home Heating Needs

System sizing, equipment placement, existing heating equipment, and your home's insulation all matter when planning a successful heat pump installation.

Program Documentation

Keep required invoices, equipment information, installation records, and other documentation. Follow the current instructions for submitting an incentive claim.

Choosing the Right Heat Pump for Your Maine Home

Maine homeowners have different heating and cooling needs. A ductless mini-split may work well for individual rooms or areas without ductwork. Ducted systems may be an option for homes with existing ductwork, while multi-zone systems can provide heating and cooling to several areas.

Proper equipment selection and installation planning are important, particularly for homes that rely on heat pumps during cold Maine winters.
