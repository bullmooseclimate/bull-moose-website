What can happen when cleaning is neglected?

Dirt and debris gradually collect on the indoor evaporator coil, blower wheel, drain pan, and outdoor coil. As buildup increases, it can restrict airflow and interfere with heat transfer. Your heat pump may lose performance, use more energy, and work harder than necessary to keep your home comfortable.

Over time, neglected buildup can contribute to drainage problems, unnecessary component strain, and premature wear. In some cases, the resulting problems can lead to avoidable repairs and may shorten the service life of the equipment.

Regular professional cleaning cannot guarantee that a system will never need repairs, but it is an important part of preventive care. It helps maintain performance, protects your investment, and gives us an opportunity to notice developing concerns.

How often should a heat pump be cleaned?

Cleaning needs vary depending on how often the system runs, household conditions, pets, dust, and the surrounding environment. If you notice visible buildup, unusual odors, reduced airflow, or declining performance, it may be time for a professional cleaning.

Everyday care between professional cleanings

Clean or check your indoor filters according to the manufacturer's instructions. Keep the outdoor unit clear of leaves, grass, snow, and other obstructions. Do not attempt to pressure-wash the equipment or disassemble internal components yourself.
