Cleaning & Maintenance

A little routine care goes a long way toward keeping your heat pump clean, efficient, and ready for every season.

Ask About Maintenance

Take care of the system that takes care of your home

Heat pumps operate throughout the year. Dust, debris, leaves, snow, and everyday use can affect how well the system performs. Regular attention helps protect your investment and maintain comfortable operation.

Clean Indoor Filters

Check indoor unit filters regularly. Clean them according to the manufacturer's instructions and allow them to dry completely before reinstalling.

Keep the Outdoor Unit Clear

Remove leaves, grass, and debris around the outdoor unit. Keep snow and ice from blocking airflow.

Watch for Changes

Pay attention to unusual sounds, odors, water leaks, reduced airflow, or changes in performance.

Keep Airflow Open

Avoid blocking indoor units, return-air grilles, or supply registers.

Routine care

Keep your system in good shape

Filters, coils, airflow, and outdoor equipment all deserve attention throughout the year.

Indoor Unit Cleaning

Filters and internal components can collect dust during normal operation.

Professional Maintenance

Professional cleaning and inspection can help address buildup and performance concerns.

Seasonal maintenance matters

In spring and summer, check filters and clear vegetation and debris around the outdoor unit. In fall and winter, keep snow and ice clear of the equipment and make sure drainage areas remain unobstructed.

When to call for service

Refrigerant work, electrical repairs, internal component repairs, and other technical service should be handled by qualified professionals.

If your system is not heating or cooling properly, or you notice unusual noises, persistent ice buildup, or water leakage, have it checked rather than attempting internal repairs.
