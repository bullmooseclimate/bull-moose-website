Why professional cleaning matters

A dirty coil or blower wheel can restrict airflow and interfere with normal heat transfer. Buildup inside the unit can also contribute to unpleasant odors and drainage problems.

Keeping the equipment clean helps support proper airflow, efficient operation, and a more comfortable home. Cleaning is not a substitute for mechanical repairs or manufacturer-recommended maintenance, but it is an important part of caring for your investment.

How often should a heat pump be cleaned?

Cleaning needs vary depending on how often the system runs, household conditions, pets, dust, and the surrounding environment. If you notice visible buildup, unusual odors, reduced airflow, or declining performance, it may be time for a professional cleaning.

Everyday care between professional cleanings

Clean or check your indoor filters according to the manufacturer's instructions. Keep the outdoor unit clear of leaves, grass, snow, and other obstructions. Do not attempt to pressure-wash the equipment or disassemble internal components yourself.
