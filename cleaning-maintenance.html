Cleaning & Maintenance

A little routine care goes a long way toward keeping your heat pump clean, efficient, and ready for every season.

Ask About Maintenance

Take care of the system that takes care of your home

Heat pumps operate throughout the year. Dust, debris, leaves, snow, and everyday use can affect how well the system performs. Regular attention helps protect your investment and maintain comfortable operation.

Clean Indoor Filters

Check your indoor unit filters regularly. Clean them according to the manufacturer's instructions and allow them to dry completely before reinstalling.

Keep the Outdoor Unit Clear

Remove leaves, grass, and other debris around the outdoor unit. Keep snow and ice from blocking airflow, while avoiding damage to the equipment.

Watch for Changes

Pay attention to unusual sounds, odors, water leaks, reduced airflow, or changes in heating and cooling performance.

Keep Airflow Open

Avoid blocking indoor units, return-air grilles, or supply registers. Good airflow helps the system operate as intended.

Seasonal maintenance matters

Your heat pump faces different conditions throughout the year. A few simple checks can help you catch problems before they become bigger concerns.

Spring & Summer

Check filters, clear vegetation and debris around the outdoor unit, and make sure the system has room to move air freely.

Fall & Winter

Keep snow and ice clear of the outdoor unit. Make sure drainage areas remain unobstructed and check filters regularly during heavier heating use.

When to call for service

Routine filter cleaning and keeping the outdoor unit clear are homeowner tasks. Refrigerant work, electrical repairs, internal component repairs, and other technical service should be handled by qualified professionals.

If your system is not heating or cooling properly, or you notice unusual noises, persistent ice buildup, or water leakage, have it checked rather than attempting internal repairs yourself.
