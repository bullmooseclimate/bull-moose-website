A better way to heat and cool your home

Heat pumps provide both heating and air conditioning by transferring heat rather than generating it through conventional electric resistance heating. They can be an efficient option for Maine homeowners looking to improve year-round comfort.

Whether you're considering a new heating system, replacing an older heat pump, or looking for an alternative to oil heat, the right equipment depends on your home's layout, insulation, existing heating system, and the rooms you want to make more comfortable.

Heat pumps for Maine's cold climate

Modern cold-climate heat pumps are designed to provide heating in low outdoor temperatures. Choosing equipment appropriate for Maine winters is an important part of planning a reliable home heating system.

Heating performance varies by model and outdoor temperature. Proper equipment selection, sizing, and installation help determine how well a system will meet your home's needs.

A heat pump may serve as a primary heating system or work alongside existing heating equipment, depending on the home and the system selected.

Choosing the right heat pump system

Square footage is only one part of selecting a heat pump. Insulation, windows, ceiling height, room layout, air leakage, and existing heating equipment all matter.

We take the time to understand your home and explain the options before recommending a system. Our goal is to help you choose equipment that makes sense for your home and your comfort needs.
